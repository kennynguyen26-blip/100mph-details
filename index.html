FAQ

Where do you service?

Katy and surrounding areas. We come to you.

How do I book?

Text 346-377-5983 or DM @100mph.details with your car and the package you want.

How do I pay?

Zelle or cash, paid when the service is complete. No cards or checks.

Why do prices say "+"?

Prices are starting prices. Bigger vehicles and heavier messes take longer, so we confirm your exact price by text before we start.

How long does it take?

Quick Detail about 1.5 to 2 hours, Full Detail about 3 to 4, Deep Clean about 4 to 6+.

Can you get out every stain?

We'll do everything we can, but some stains and odors can't be fully removed. We'll tell you up front.

What do you need from me?

Access to the vehicle and a spot to work. Please remove valuables beforehand. We'll confirm anything else by text.

What if I need to reschedule?

Text us as early as you can, ideally 24 hours ahead.
