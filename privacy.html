Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this website collects

This website has no forms and collects no personal information directly. The quote builder runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into it is sent to us unless you choose to send the message yourself by text or Instagram.

When you text or DM us

When you text us or send us a direct message on Instagram, we receive what you send. That can include your name, phone number, location and vehicle details. We use it only to quote, schedule and provide your service.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. We don't share it except as needed to provide the service or to comply with the law.

Hosting, fonts and scripts

This website is hosted by Vercel. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation code from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These providers may log basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser requests their files.

Deleting your messages

To have your messages deleted, contact us at details.100mph@outlook.com.
